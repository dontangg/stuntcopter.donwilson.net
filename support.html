Support

StuntCopter is an arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Robert Wilson. Here's how to get help or tell me what you think.

Bugs, questions and ideas

Open an issue on the StuntCopter issue list. Tell me which device you play on and, for a problem, what happened just before it. You can also see what others have reported. You'll need a free GitHub account, and everything there is public, so don't include any personal information.
