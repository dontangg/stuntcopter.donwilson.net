Fly the helicopter. Drop the stunt man. Hope he lands in the hay wagon.

Free for iPhone and iPad · Coming back to Google Play

Haven't you always wanted to fly a helicopter, then jump out without a parachute, and hope you land in a hay wagon? Tilt your device to fly, and tap to send the stunt man flying. If he lands on the hay, you score and keep going. If he misses, it's game over. It's simple, and hard to put down.

How to play

  1. Fly. Tilt your device left and right to move the helicopter.
  2. Jump. Tap the screen to send the stunt man out of the helicopter.
  3. Land in the hay. Hit the hay wagon to score. Miss and hit the ground, the horse or the driver, and the game's over.
  4. Score big. A longer fall scores more, and you get bonus points for falling through a cloud, for moving fast when you land, and for reaching higher levels.

It gets faster…

Every five landings is a new level. First the horse speeds up:

  1. Walk
  2. Trot
  3. Canter
  4. Gallop

…then lighter

After that, gravity eases off, and the stunt man drifts further every time:

  1. Heavy
  2. Normal
  3. Oh boy!
  4. Flying
